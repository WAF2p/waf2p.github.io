The mark
--------
The wombat wears a hard hat because the work is foundations:
controls, evidence, exit strategies. The head is the mark; the
long-form story illustrations are supporting art.

Keep the hard hat yellow (#FFCF63), the outline dark (#3A2A22) and
the fur brown (#8B5A3C). Do not recolor, stretch, rotate or crop
the mark. Leave one brim-height of clear space around the head.
Do not put the wombat in a suit. It is a builder.

The pillar icons
----------------
24×24 grid, 1.75 stroke, currentColor. Use them for framework
concepts and UI; do not put the mascot inside an icon.
