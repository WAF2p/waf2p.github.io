What each data point comes from.

Every provider page is a compilation of public project data, not a provider-issued certification.

Provider list

Dashboard selector + region map

The nine concrete providers in the WOMBATpass dashboard provider selector, plus the European providers recognised by the dashboard region map. “Multi-cloud” and “other” are metadata modes, not providers.

Control counts

Current framework control pack

Counts are unique control pages with provider-specific checks in the current framework control pack. A control may have multiple checks; the count is not a certification or a guarantee of full resource coverage.

Region labels

Dashboard region map

Approximate region labels and coordinates from the WOMBATpass dashboard region map. They are city/region level, not datacenter addresses, and are not cross-validated with the providers.

Refresh

Static snapshots

The data is committed to the site and refreshed by maintainers. {% if site.data.clouds.region_data_updated %}The current region extract is dated {{ site.data.clouds.region_data_updated | date: "%d %b %Y" }}.{% endif %} A date records the extraction, not a guarantee that every region is current.

What this data is not.

The pages are an orientation aid. Treat them as a starting point for verification, not as an availability or compliance guarantee.

  • Not a datacenter map. Region labels are approximate city/region markers. They do not disclose exact facilities, addresses or availability-zone topology.
  • Not cross-validated. We do not claim that every region is current, complete or confirmed by the provider.
  • Not a certification. Control counts describe the current control pack; they are not an audit opinion, certification or statement of full coverage.
  • Not legal advice. Jurisdiction, residency and procurement questions must be validated with the provider and your legal team.
  • No provider logos. Provider names are used for identification only. Trademarks belong to their respective owners; Wombat is not affiliated with or endorsed by the providers.

How to submit a correction.

Providers, customers and maintainers can correct the data. Please include the provider, the region code or control count, the correction and a public source.

Report a provider data correction

Send the provider name, the affected region or control, the correct value and a public source. We will review it and update the snapshot.
